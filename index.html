A/B test analysis of a 14M-user ad experiment

+59% conversion lift (95% CI 54–65%)

I validated the randomization, cut variance 25% with cross-fitted CUPED and confirmed the lift with a Bayesian model. Separating intent-to-treat from the effect on people who actually saw ads showed a naive comparison overstated the impact by a third. It ends in a break-even decision memo.

Movie recommender: four models, one honest test

HitRate@10 1.4% → 13.5%, 9x the popularity baseline

On 1M MovieLens ratings, I hid each user's most recent movie and asked four models to find it in their top 10. EASE, a closed-form linear model, matched matrix factorization and recommended from 47% of the catalog, where popularity used under 1%.
